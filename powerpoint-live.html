PollSlide LIVE · Help & support

Live audience results, right on your PowerPoint slide

PollSlide LIVE is a PowerPoint add-in that sits on a slide like a picture or a chart. Your audience answers on their phones, and the results on the slide update in real time — including during your slide show.

Works in PowerPoint on Windows, Mac and the web · free individual accounts — no enterprise purchase, licence key or admin setup

1. Get a PollSlide account

You need a PollSlide account to choose which question a slide shows. Anyone can create one for free at app.pollslide.com/presenter in about a minute — no credit card, no licence key and no administrator approval. Then create a presentation and add a few questions: a poll, a quiz, a survey, a word cloud or a rating.

Your audience never needs an account or an app. They scan a QR code or type a short code at pollslide.com/join.

2. Put PollSlide LIVE on a slide

  1. In PowerPoint, open the slide where you want the results.
  2. Go to Insert → Add-ins (on the web: Insert → Add-ins, then My Add-ins) and choose PollSlide LIVE.
  3. It appears on the slide as a box. Move and resize it like any picture — full width, or a narrow column beside your content.
If your organisation manages Office add-ins, your IT administrator may need to allow PollSlide LIVE first.

3. Choose the question for this slide

  1. Click the box and sign in with your PollSlide account.
  2. Pick a presentation, then the question this slide should show.
  3. The box now shows that question and its live results. Each slide can show a different question — put a PollSlide LIVE box on each one.

While you are editing, small buttons in the corner of the box let you change the text size (A− / A+), show or hide answer images (🖼), switch between light and dark (🌙) to match your slide, or Change question. They are never shown during the slide show.

4. Present

  • Start your slide show as usual. The box keeps showing the results and updates as answers arrive.
  • Put the question's QR code on the slide so people can answer: in PollSlide, open the question's live view, click Copy QR box for slide, and paste it next to the results.
  • Reveal: if the question has a right answer, it is revealed when the countdown ends — or click Reveal now. The right answer turns green.
  • Polls show animated bars, word clouds show the words people send, ratings show the average, and open questions list the latest answers.
  • If you chose to hide results until the reveal in PollSlide, the slide shows only how many people have answered until then.

Troubleshooting

The box says “Not linked to a question”

Leave the slide show, click the box in editing view and choose a question. It remembers the choice from then on.

A copied slide shows the wrong question

In PowerPoint on the web, a copied or pasted slide can pick up the question of the original. Click the box in editing view and choose the question again.

The slide show won't move to the next slide

Clicking inside the box during a slide show gives it the keyboard focus. Click anywhere outside the box, then use your clicker or arrow keys as usual.

The box stays on “Loading…”

PollSlide LIVE needs an internet connection. Check you are online, then close and reopen the presentation.

Nobody's answers appear

Make sure the QR code on the slide belongs to the same question as the box, and that people can reach pollslide.com/join from their phones.

Your data

PollSlide LIVE shows results from your PollSlide account. It saves only which question each box shows and how it looks, inside your presentation file. Signing in uses your PollSlide account; your password is never stored in the presentation. Audience answers are handled as described in our privacy policy. Use of PollSlide is covered by our terms.
