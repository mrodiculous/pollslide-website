🤝 When Kahoot might be the better fit

For a high-energy, game-show quiz — especially with younger students or a trivia crowd — Kahoot's format is purpose-built and delightful. PollSlide is the better fit when you also need real polls, open feedback, surveys or study sets, and want it all over your own professional deck.

Questions people ask

Is PollSlide a good Kahoot alternative?

Yes, if you need more than a game-show quiz. PollSlide runs quizzes with game modes alongside real polls, open feedback, surveys and study sets, over your own Keynote, PowerPoint or Google Slides deck. For a pure high-energy quiz game, especially with younger students, Kahoot is purpose-built.

Can I keep using my PowerPoint or Keynote slides?

Yes. Paste your poll's QR code onto any slide and your audience answers on their phones. On a Mac, the free PollSlide companion floats live results over your slideshow — Keynote, PowerPoint or a PDF — and shows the right poll when that slide is on screen.

Can Kahoot use my existing slides?

Kahoot! 360 can import PowerPoint, Keynote and PDF files, and its Pro Standard plan and above sync with PowerPoint and Google Slides, according to its pricing page. PollSlide works over the original deck itself: paste a QR on any slide, and the Mac companion floats live results over Keynote, PowerPoint or a PDF.

How do PollSlide and Kahoot prices compare?

PollSlide has a free plan and Pro at $12 a month ($10 billed annually), covering polls, surveys, quizzes and study sets. Kahoot! 360 Pro plans start at $19 a month billed annually, with higher tiers for more participants. Prices checked 7 October 2026.

Does my audience need to download an app?

No — with either tool. In PollSlide, people scan the QR or go to pollslide.com/join and answer in their phone's browser, with no app and no account.
