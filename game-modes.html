👥 Proper team play

Each table creates a team and everyone answers on their own phone. A team's score is the average of its members, so a table of six has no advantage over a table of two.

🎪 Warm-up while they arrive

The big screen runs light trivia before you start, so the room is entertained — and looking at your join code — while stragglers get a drink.

📱 No app to install

Players scan a QR or type a code at pollslide.com/join. Works on any phone, and the questions appear in their own language automatically.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — the free plan runs real quizzes with all five game modes, teams and the recap. Paid plans lift the limits on audience size, saved quizzes and AI question generation. See pricing.

Do players need to download anything?

No. They scan the QR on your screen or go to pollslide.com/join and type the code. It's a normal web page — any phone, no account needed.

Can I run it in a noisy pub with bad wifi?

Players need a data connection, but the pages are small and answers are tiny. Show the join code on screen the whole time so anyone who drops out can rejoin instantly.

Can I mix modes in one night?

Yes — that's the intended way to use it. Start Classic, run a Speed round in the middle, and finish on Survival for the finale. You pick the mode each time you press Present.

How is this different from Kahoot?

Kahoot is a great game-show quiz. PollSlide does gamified quizzes and polls, surveys and study sets, over your real slides, with team scoring that isn't biased toward big teams. See the honest side-by-side.

What if my questions are in another language?

Your audience picks their language on their own phone and the questions are translated for them, in context — so a mixed room can all play the same quiz.
