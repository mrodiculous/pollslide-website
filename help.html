Live polls that play right over your slides.

PollSlide turns any presentation into a two-way conversation. Build questions in minutes, drop a QR code on your slide, and your audience answers from their phones — results appear instantly. This guide walks you through everything, step by step.

🗳️ Polls📋 Surveys🏆 Quizzes📚 Study

👋 Welcome — the 3-step idea

Every PollSlide presentation works the same way, whether it's a poll, survey, quiz, or study set.

1 · Build

Create a presentation and add questions — type them yourself or let AI draft them for you.

2 · Stamp your slide

Each question has a QR code. Drop it on your Keynote, PowerPoint, or a PDF.

3 · Go live

Present. Your audience scans the QR, answers, and results animate in real time.

🗳️ Create your first poll

From a blank account to a ready question in under a minute.

  1. Sign in at app.pollslide.com.
  2. Pick a product tab — for this example, 🗳️ Polls.
  3. Click + New Poll. A fresh presentation is created.
  4. Give it a name (e.g. "Team Trivia Night") and save.
  5. You're ready to add questions — see below.
Tip: On the Free plan you can keep 3 presentations. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited polls, surveys, quizzes, and study sets.

➕ Add a question

  1. Click + Add Question.
  2. Type your question in the question box.
  3. Fill in the answer options (A, B, C, D…).
  4. Choose a question type — single choice, multi-select, rating, word cloud, or free text.
  5. Mark the correct answer (for quizzes) by tapping the ✓ next to an option.
In a hurry? Click ✨ Ask Polly and give it a topic or paste your notes — Polly drafts a full set of questions for you to edit.

✨ Draft it with Polly AI

Polly is PollSlide's built-in AI. Give it a topic — or paste your notes, an outline, or a paragraph — and it writes a whole set of questions in seconds. It works for every product: polls, surveys, quizzes and study sets.

  1. Click ✨ Ask Polly on any presentation, or ✨ New with Polly to start a brand-new one from scratch.
  2. Type what you want — e.g. "5 trivia questions about the solar system" — or paste your own notes to turn into questions.
  3. Pick how many questions (1–30) and, for images, a style.
  4. Polly drafts the questions, options, and correct answers. Edit anything before you go live.

🗳️ Any product

Polly matches what you're building — opinion polls, multi-question surveys, graded quizzes with correct answers, or flashcard study sets.

⚡ Fast & private

Polly runs on PollSlide's own servers first for speed, with a cloud fallback. Your prompts are used only to draft your questions.

Fair-use limits: Polly is metered per month by plan (Free includes a monthly allowance; Pro and Team include much more). If you hit the limit, you can still build questions by hand, or upgrade for more. See Plans & billing.

🖼️ Add an image with AI

  1. Open a question and find the image row.
  2. Click ✨ Generate with AI — PollSlide creates an image from your question text.
  3. Pick a style (Illustration, Realistic, 3D, Cartoon, Minimal, Watercolor).
  4. Prefer your own? Click ⬆ Upload or paste an image / GIF / video URL.
  5. Use the S / M / L / ⛶ buttons to size the media on the slide.

⛶ Present

Present mode shows your question full-screen with a QR code your audience scans to answer.

  1. Click ⛶ Present on any question.
  2. Your audience points their phone camera at the QR code — no app needed.
  3. Watch responses roll in live. Switch chart styles anytime.
  4. Use 📋 Copy QR box for slide to paste the QR straight onto your own Keynote / PowerPoint / any deck.

📱 No phone to scan the QR? No problem.

Anyone on a laptop (or any browser) can join without scanning: they go to pollslide.com/join and type the code shown next to your QR. Same session, same live results.

So your audience always has both options, add this line under the QR on your slide:
📱 Scan the QR — or go to pollslide.com/join and enter code ABC123
In the app, the Share / QR window has a “Copy this line” button that fills in your real code for you.

🔗 Share without presenting

Not every project is presented live. Surveys, quizzes and study sets can be sent out and answered at your audience's own pace — no QR on a slide required.

  1. Open your presentation and click 🔗 Share.
  2. Copy the join link (or its QR code) and post it anywhere — a chat, an LMS, a slide, a printout.
  3. Or click ✉️ Email it to send the link straight to a list of recipients.
  4. Prefer a code? Anyone can go to pollslide.com/join and type the 6-character code.

📋 Surveys

Collect feedback over hours or days. Every response flows into the same live results and CSV export.

📚 Study sets

Learners review flashcards on their own with spaced repetition — share the link and they practice anytime.

One link, one tally: whether people scan a QR live, join by code on a laptop, or open an emailed link, they all land in the same session — your results stay in one place.

📊 Post-reveal & results

When you reveal the answer, PollSlide celebrates with leaderboards, podiums, insight chips, and confetti.

  1. In the editor, choose a post-reveal style: Leaderboard, Podium, Scorecard, or an Explainer / Did-you-know card.
  2. Set how long after the first answer to auto-reveal (or reveal manually).
  3. Add optional explainer text shown after the reveal.
  4. Turn on auto-advance to flow to the next question hands-free.

🎉 Reveal live

  1. Click Reveal now (or let the timer auto-reveal).
  2. The correct answer highlights in green; results lock.
  3. The leaderboard / podium appears with names and response times.
  4. Advance to the next question and keep the momentum going.

📊 Results, reports & export

Every response is saved. Review results live during your talk, or come back to them any time afterward — and take the raw data with you.

  1. During a session, watch results update live — bars, donuts, word clouds, leaderboards and podiums.
  2. Open 📊 Report on any presentation for the full Response Report: totals, per-question breakdowns, and per-participant answers.
  3. Click ⬇ CSV to export everything to a spreadsheet — great for grading, analysis, or your records.
  4. Running the same quiz more than once? Every reset is saved with its date, so past sessions never disappear.

🖥️ Big screen

Click 🖥️ Big screen to open a clean, full-screen results view for a projector or second monitor — no companion app needed.

🔒 Your data

Exports are yours to keep. Delete a session or your whole account any time — see Your data & privacy.

✨ AI Insights — read the room

When your audience answers an open-text question, Polly can read every response and show you the themes, the mood, and a one-line summary — instantly, on your results panel. Perfect for "What's on your mind?", "One word for today", or any free-form question.

  1. Run any open text or word cloud question and let answers roll in — you need at least 2.
  2. On your live results panel, click ✨ AI Insights.
  3. Polly returns a summary, an overall sentiment bar (positive / neutral / negative), and the top themes — each with a count and a real example quote.
  4. Click it again any time to re-analyze as more answers arrive.

🧠 Themes, not noise

Hundreds of replies become a ranked list of what people actually said — grouped, counted, with an example each.

🔒 Private & included

Polly analyzes on PollSlide's own servers first — not shipped to a third-party AI cloud — and it's included in your plan.

📊 Works in your Report too

The same ✨ AI Insights button sits on every open-text question in your Report — so you can analyze feedback and survey answers long after the session ends.

Tip: AI Insights shines on open-ended questions. For multiple-choice, your live bars and the Report already show the full breakdown.

📚 Study it yourself — and make it a game

StudySlide isn't only for handing out to a class. If you bought PollSlide to learn something yourself, you can turn any deck into flashcards and revise it with spaced repetition, a daily streak and four study modes — including two built like game shows.

  1. On a study set, click 📚 Study now in the top bar — it opens straight into your own cards. No link to copy, no sharing required.
  2. Got a quiz or poll instead? Click 📚 Study this deck and PollSlide turns every question into a flashcard (question on the front, correct answer on the back).
  3. You land on your study home: your streak, how many cards are due, and a mastery bar.
  4. Pick how you want to study today — then just keep coming back. The scheduler decides what you see next.

🎯 Review

Flip cards and rate yourself Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Proper spaced repetition schedules each card for the moment you're about to forget it.

⚡ Beat the Clock

Sixty seconds, as many cards as you can get right, and a personal best to beat. Perfect when you've only got two minutes.

🪜 Climb the Ladder

Ten rungs of rising stakes with two lifelines and safe havens — bank your progress or risk it for the top. Game-show tension, real revision.

🎲 Pick-a-Card Board

Choose squares from a board. The high-value squares are quietly the cards you know least well, so the game steers you at your weak spots.

🔥 Daily streak

Study anything, any set, and your streak grows. Miss a day and it resets — which is exactly why it works.

🏆 Mastery bar

Every card moves Learning → Familiar → Mastered as it survives longer gaps. Watching the bar go green is the point.

Every mode is real study. The games use the same cards and feed the same spaced-repetition scheduler as Review — a right answer still pushes the card further out, a wrong one still brings it back sooner. Playing is never wasted time.

🎮 Game modes — pick how your quiz plays

When you hit Present, PollSlide asks how you want to run it. Classic works exactly as it always has. The other four change how points are scored and what the big screen celebrates — same questions, completely different energy in the room.

  1. Build your questions as normal, then click ⛶ Present.
  2. Pick a mode in the How do you want to run this? box. Classic is already selected, so hit Start presenting if you just want the usual.
  3. Everyone's phone shows what's at stake, and the big screen calls out the drama as each answer is revealed.
  4. You can switch modes between rounds — start Classic, finish on Survival for a grand finale.

🎯 Classic

One point per correct answer, you reveal when you're ready. Exactly how PollSlide has always worked — nothing changes. Best for: any audience.

⚡ Speed round

The faster you answer correctly, the more you score — up to double points for the quickest. The reveal crowns a Fastest Finger. Best for: parties and lifting a flat room.

🔥 Streak

Correct answers in a row build a multiplier up to 3×, with flames that grow as you go. One miss resets it. Best for: classrooms and training — it rewards consistency.

💀 Survival

One wrong answer and you're out. Knocked-out players keep watching, and the screen counts down who's still standing. Best for: a dramatic final round.

🎲 Wager

Before each question players bet Safe (1×), Risky (2×) or Bold (3×). Get it wrong and it costs you. Best for: pub-quiz joker rounds and comebacks.

📈 Everyone can see they're moving

Whatever the mode, the leaderboard shows ▲ and ▼ arrows as people climb and slip — so the room feels the race, not just the scores.

Nothing else changes. Your questions, timers, reveal, translations, team play, reports and CSV export all work identically in every mode — the mode only affects scoring and the celebration.

👥 Team play (pub-quiz mode)

Turn any quiz into a proper pub quiz: the audience answers as teams at their tables, and the leaderboard ranks teams instead of individuals. Everyone still answers on their own phone — the scores just add up per team.

  1. Open the presentation you want to run and click 👥 Teams: Off in the top bar — it flips to Teams: On.
  2. Present as normal. After people type their name, they see a Join a team screen.
  3. The first person at a table taps Create team and names it ("The Quizzly Bears"). Everyone else on that table sees it in the list and taps to join.
  4. Anyone who'd rather play alone taps Play solo — they still appear on the leaderboard as themselves.
  5. Your leaderboards, podium and Final Results now show team standings.

⚖️ Big teams don't win by default

A team's score is the average of its members, so a table of six has no advantage over a table of two. It rewards knowing the answers, not recruiting.

📱 One phone each

Everyone answers on their own phone — no passing a single device around, and everyone gets the questions in their own language if they need to.

🔀 Latecomers are fine

Turn team play on at any point. Anyone already in gets the team screen next time they answer, and people can join a team late.

Tip: Ask each table to agree their team name before you start question 1 — it saves a minute and gets everyone laughing early.

🎪 Waiting-room warm-up

Instead of a static "waiting for the presenter" screen while people trickle in, the big screen can run light-hearted trivia — so the room is entertained (and looking at your join code) before you've even started.

  1. Open the 🖥️ Big screen view from the top bar (that's the projector view with the join code and QR).
  2. Warm-up trivia appears automatically underneath the join banner while you're waiting.
  3. Questions rotate on their own — each shows for a few seconds, reveals its answer, then moves on.
  4. It disappears the instant you launch your first real question.
  5. Don't want it? Click 🎪 Warm-up: On in the bottom corner to switch it off — that screen remembers your choice.

🚫 No spoilers

Warm-up questions come from a built-in general-knowledge set — never from your deck — so nothing in your actual quiz is given away.

👀 Keeps the code on screen

The join link, code and QR stay visible above the trivia, so latecomers can still get in while everyone else plays along.

⚡ Zero setup

Nothing to configure and no AI needed — it works even if your internet or AI service is having a bad day.

🎯 Polly Live Co-pilot

Mid-session, Polly can read how your audience just answered and suggest a follow-up question that responds to it — which you can put on screen with one click. It's like having a co-host who noticed the room split 60/40 and knows what to ask next.

  1. Launch a question and let a few answers come in (you need at least 3).
  2. On your live results panel, click 🎯 Suggest a follow-up.
  3. Polly reads the actual responses and offers 3 ready-made questions, each with four options, the correct answer marked where there is one, and a short note on why it's suggesting that one.
  4. Pick one: ⚡ Add & launch now puts it live immediately, or ＋ Add to deck saves it for later.
  5. Not useful? Close it. Nothing is added or launched unless you choose it.

🧭 You're always in control

Polly never launches anything by itself. Every suggestion is a proposal you accept, edit later, or ignore.

🔒 Private & included

Like AI Insights, this runs on PollSlide's own AI first rather than a third-party cloud, and it's included in your plan — not charged per use.

🛟 Never blocks your session

If the AI can't be reached, you get a calm "unavailable right now" message. Your questions, results and reveal all carry on exactly as normal.

Works with both kinds of question: for multiple-choice it reads the vote split; for open text it reads what people actually wrote.

🎁 Shareable session recap

When a session ends, build a recap page in one click and send the link to everyone who took part — the winner, the question that fooled the room, and the highlights. Great for "same time next week?" and it works long after the session is over.

  1. Open 📊 Report from the top bar.
  2. In the filters, choose the presentation you want to recap (one at a time — that's what the recap covers).
  3. Click 🎁 Share recap.
  4. You get a link — Copy link to send it round, or Open recap to see it first.
  5. Anyone with the link can open it. No sign-in, no app, works on any phone.

🏆 The bits people care about

Winner (or winning team), leaderboard, how many took part, the toughest question, and a question-by-question breakdown.

✨ With a summary when it fits

If your session had written answers, Polly adds a one-line "in a nutshell" summary. If the AI isn't available, the recap still publishes with every stat intact.

🔗 Link-only, and yours to delete

The page is only reachable by its unguessable link — it isn't listed or searchable. It shows the display names people chose during the session.

Tip: Post the recap link in your group chat right after the quiz — it's the easiest way to get the same people back next time.

📁 Folders, search & pinning

Once you've built more than a handful of projects, finding the right one matters more than making the next one. Folders group a whole lesson together — whatever mix of products it contains — and search looks inside your questions, not just their titles.

  1. In the left panel, the ribbon under the product tabs shows where you are: 📁 All › Biology 101 › Unit 3. Every step is clickable.
  2. Click 📁 New folder at the bottom to create one. Folders go two levels deep — enough for Course › Unit without ever getting lost.
  3. To file something, open a deck's ⋯ menu and choose Move to folder… — or tick several with ☑ and move them all at once.
  4. Anything you create while inside a folder is filed there automatically.
  5. The All tab shows every product together. The Poll / Survey / Quiz / Study tabs now filter whatever folder you're in, rather than sending you to a separate screen.

🔍 Search finds questions, not just names

Type a word from inside a question and it finds the deck — from any folder, and it tells you which one it's in. You don't have to remember where you filed it.

★ Pin what you run weekly

Pinned decks sit at the top no matter how you're sorting. Perfect for the warm-up you use every Monday.

📦 Archive, don't delete

Put last term away without losing it. Archived decks leave your lists but keep every answer, and come back whenever you want them.

Why folders ignore product type: a single lesson usually needs a warm-up poll, a graded quiz and a revision set. Filing by folder instead of by type is what lets those three sit side by side.

📤 Share a deck with someone

Send a colleague a copy of any poll, survey, quiz or study set. They don't need to be on your team, don't need the same plan, and don't even need a PollSlide account yet — the share waits for them.

  1. Open the deck's ⋯ menu in the left panel and choose 📤 Share with someone…
  2. Type their email address and add a note if it needs context.
  3. Click 📤 Send copy. They get an email, and the share appears in their PollSlide under 📥 Shared with me.
  4. When they accept, a copy lands in their library. They can rewrite every question and run it with their own audience.
  5. Your original never changes — and neither does their copy if you edit yours afterwards.

📥 Shared with me

A button appears at the bottom of your deck list whenever something is waiting. Accept it, or decline — nothing is added to your library until you say so.

👤 They don't need an account yet

Share to any email. If they've never used PollSlide, it's there the first time they sign in with that address.

🔒 It's a copy, not a live link

Nobody gets access to your account or your other work — just the one deck, as a snapshot, at the moment you sent it.

Sent something by mistake? A pending share can be withdrawn before it's accepted. Once it's been accepted the copy belongs to them, and there's no taking it back — so check the address before you send.

🖥️ The Mac Companion

PollSlide Companion is a free macOS app that floats your live results over any presentation — it even auto-detects the on-screen QR code and shows the matching poll. Download it free →

🔗 Set up the Mac Companion — step by step

Follow these seven steps once and you're set for every presentation. It takes about two minutes. Requirements: a Mac on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later — it's free.

1 Download it

Go to pollslide.com/download and click Download for Mac. A file called PollSlideCompanion.dmg lands in your Downloads. It's signed & notarized by Apple.

2 Open the disk image

In your Downloads, double-click PollSlideCompanion.dmg. A little install window opens.

3 Drag it into Applications

In the window that opens, drag the PollSlide Companion icon onto the Applications folder beside it.

⚠ Don't skip this
Running it straight from the disk image or Downloads stops macOS from remembering the Screen-Recording permission — so auto-detection won't work. It must live in Applications.

4 Open it the first time

Open your Applications folder and double-click PollSlide Companion. macOS asks once whether you're sure — click Open. A small chart icon 📊 appears in your menu bar (top-right).

5 Allow Screen Recording

The app asks for Screen Recording — this is how it spots the QR code on your slide. It opens Settings for you: turn the switch ON for PollSlide Companion, then Quit & Reopen the app when macOS offers.

⚠ Needed for auto-detect
It's used only to read your PollSlide QR — nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

6 Pair with your account

On pollslide.com, open your account menu → Connect Mac App to get a 6-character code. Click the menu-bar chart icon 📊 → Connect Mac App, and type that code in. Done — you're linked.

7 Dry run, then present

Present your slides as usual — the results window pops up on its own whenever your QR slide is on screen, and hides when you move on. Toggle it anytime with Control-Option-Y.

Using a projector or second screen? Do a quick dry run first: if the window opens on the wrong display, drag it by its top bar onto the screen your slides are on — it stays there for the whole talk.
Still not popping up on your QR slide? 99% of the time it's one of two things: (1) the app isn't in your Applications folder — move it there (Step 3); or (2) Screen Recording is off, or the app needs a restart after you turned it on — check Step 5, then quit the app (menu-bar icon → Quit) and reopen it. Make sure you're on version 1.3.3 or later.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The quick fixes for the handful of things that trip people up.

The companion isn't popping up on my QR slide

99% of the time it's one of two things: (1) the app isn't in your Applications folder — move it there; or (2) Screen Recording is off, or the app needs a restart after you enabled it. Turn it on in System Settings, then quit the app from its menu-bar icon and reopen it. Make sure you're on the latest version from pollslide.com/download.

My audience can't join or results aren't updating

PollSlide needs an internet connection on both your device and your audience's. Check you're online, then have them re-scan the QR or go to pollslide.com/join and enter the code. Refresh the presenter page if a session looks stuck.

The QR code won't scan

Make it bigger on your slide and keep good contrast (dark on light). Most phone cameras scan automatically — if not, any QR app works, or use the pollslide.com/join + code option instead.

Polly AI is slow or didn't generate

Try again — the first request after a while can be slower. If you've hit your monthly Polly allowance, you'll be told; you can still add questions by hand or upgrade for more. See Plans & billing.

The results window opened on the wrong screen

Drag it by its top bar onto the display your slides are on — it stays there for the rest of your talk. With a projector or second display, always do a quick dry run first so you can position it once.

My study streak reset even though I studied

The streak counts calendar days and only banks when you finish a session, so closing the tab halfway through won't count. It's also per-device (it lives in that browser), so studying on your phone and laptop keeps two separate streaks. Note it's now a single streak across all your sets — studying any set keeps it alive.

Study progress vanished / cards reset to Learning

Study progress is stored in that browser, not your account — so clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching device starts fresh. The cards themselves are safe; only the schedule and streak are local.

I picked a game mode but scores look normal

Check the mode actually took: the picker appears each time you press Present, and whatever is selected there is what runs. If people joined before you switched modes, ask them to reload the answer page. Also note Classic and the other modes score the same on a single question — the differences build up across a round. See Game modes.

Someone was knocked out in Survival but wants back in

Survival is deliberately final within a round — that's what creates the tension. To give everyone a fresh start, finish the round and start the next one (or reset the tally from the presenter), and they'll be back in from the next question.

The team screen didn't appear for my audience

Check that 👥 Teams: On is showing in the top bar for that presentation — the setting is per presentation, not global. Anyone who joined before you switched it on will see the team screen the next time they answer a question. See Team play.

Someone joined the wrong team

They can pick again: ask them to close the answer page and re-scan the QR (or reopen the link). They'll get the team list again and their later answers count for the new team.

🎯 Suggest a follow-up says the co-pilot is unavailable

That means the AI service couldn't be reached right then — it's the safe fallback, not a broken session. Everything else (questions, results, reveal, leaderboards) carries on completely as normal. Try again in a moment, or check the status page.

My recap link doesn't work / says expired

Recap links are long and unguessable — make sure the whole link was copied, including the part after the #. Chat apps sometimes cut long links; paste it as plain text or use the Copy link button again from your Report. See Shareable session recap.

Still stuck?

Email help@pollslide.com and check the status page to see if everything's running normally.

❓ FAQ

Does my audience need to install anything?

No. They just scan the QR code with their phone camera and answer in the browser.

Can I use PollSlide with Keynote, PowerPoint or a PDF?

Yes — any of them. Use Copy QR box for slide to paste the QR onto any slide, or run the Mac Companion to float live results over whatever's on your screen — Keynote, PowerPoint, a PDF, even a webpage. There's also a PowerPoint add-in.

How do I show results on the projector?

Use Present mode in the browser, or run the Mac Companion app for a floating results window that stays on top of your slides.

What's the difference between Reveal and Post-reveal?

Reveal locks responses and highlights the correct answer. Post-reveal is what shows next — a leaderboard, podium, scorecard, or explainer card.

Why does the companion ask for Screen Recording?

Only to read the QR code on your current slide so it shows the right poll automatically. Nothing is captured or sent.

Can I use PollSlide to study something myself?

Yes. Open a study set and click 📚 Study now — it opens your own cards with spaced repetition, a daily streak and four study modes. If what you have is a quiz or poll, click 📚 Study this deck to turn it into flashcards first. See Study it yourself.

Do the study games actually help me learn, or are they just fun?

Both. Every study mode uses the same cards and feeds the same spaced-repetition scheduler as normal review — getting one right pushes it further into the future, getting it wrong brings it back sooner. Playing Beat the Clock is real revision, not a break from it.

Can I make the quiz more of a game?

Yes — when you hit Present, pick a game mode. Speed round rewards fast answers, Streak builds a multiplier, Survival knocks players out one wrong answer at a time, and Wager lets people bet on themselves. Classic stays exactly as it always was. See Game modes.

Do game modes change my questions?

No. The questions, options, timers, reveal, translations and reports are all identical — a mode only changes how points are counted and what the big screen celebrates. You can switch modes between rounds.

Can people answer in teams, like a pub quiz?

Yes — switch on 👥 Teams for that presentation and everyone picks or creates a team when they join. Team score is the average of its members, so bigger teams don't win automatically. See Team play.

Does the AI cost extra to use during a session?

No. AI Insights and the Live Co-pilot are included in your plan and aren't charged per use — they run on PollSlide's own AI first. Only generating questions with Polly counts against your monthly allowance. See Plans & billing.

What happens to my session if the AI is down?

Nothing important. Questions, answers, live results, reveal, leaderboards, reports and CSV export are all completely independent of AI. Only the optional extras (Co-pilot suggestions, Insights, the recap's summary line) pause, and they say so clearly.

Can I send people the results afterwards?

Yes — open your Report and click 🎁 Share recap to get a link anyone can open on their phone, with the winner and highlights. See Shareable session recap.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lets you build and run real polls, surveys, quizzes and study sets, with a monthly Polly AI allowance — no credit card required. Upgrade only when you need unlimited presentations, larger audiences, or more Polly. See Plans & billing.

How many people can join a poll?

It depends on your plan — the Free plan is capped for smaller rooms, and paid plans raise the limit substantially. The pricing page shows the current participant caps for each plan.

Do I need to download anything?

No download is required to build or present — everything runs in your browser at app.pollslide.com. The Mac Companion is an optional free app that floats live results over your slides; your audience never installs anything either.

Can I use PollSlide in another language?

Yes. The interface and this site auto-detect your browser language (English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese and Italian), and your audience's answer screen is translated too. You can also switch language manually.

Can I present online over Zoom, Meet or Teams?

Absolutely. Share your screen as usual and present from the PollSlide app, or run the Mac Companion so live results float over whatever you're screen-sharing. Remote audiences scan the on-screen QR or join at pollslide.com/join.

Who owns the data and responses?

You do. Export everything to CSV any time, and delete a session or your entire account whenever you like. See Your data & privacy.
