PollSlide vs Poll Everywhere

Poll Everywhere has run live classroom and meeting polls since 2007, with deep Canvas gradebook integration and SMS voting built in. PollSlide takes a different route: one QR you paste onto any slide, in any tool, plus AI that drafts from your own material and a full quiz-and-study suite in one plan.

The short version

Poll Everywhere is a mature, institutional tool — its Canvas gradebook integration and SMS text-in fallback are genuinely hard to match, especially for large campus lecture classes. PollSlide is built for presenters who don't want to insert activity slides into their deck at all: paste one QR box onto any existing slide, run polls, surveys, quizzes and study sets from a single plan, and let Polly AI draft from a PDF or your own notes instead of just a typed topic.

🤝 When Poll Everywhere might be the better fit

Poll Everywhere has been refining live classroom and meeting polling since 2007, and it shows: campuses standardized on Canvas get native gradebook and roster integration that PollSlide doesn't offer, and SMS text-in voting is a genuine fallback for audiences without reliable phone data or the ability to install anything. If that institutional depth — or a text-in option — is what you need, Poll Everywhere is worth a look. PollSlide is the better fit when you want polls, quizzes, surveys and study in one plan, AI that works from your own material, and nothing to rebuild when you switch presentation tools.

Questions people ask

Is PollSlide a good Poll Everywhere alternative?

Yes, if you want polls, surveys, quizzes and study sets in one plan, plus AI that turns open answers into themes. Poll Everywhere is a long-established choice with SMS text-in voting and deep LMS integration, which PollSlide does not offer.

Can I keep using my PowerPoint or Keynote slides?

Yes. Paste your poll's QR code onto any slide and your audience answers on their phones. On a Mac, the free PollSlide companion floats live results over your slideshow — Keynote, PowerPoint or a PDF — and shows the right poll when that slide is on screen.

Does Poll Everywhere work inside PowerPoint and Keynote?

Yes. Poll Everywhere lists PowerPoint integration on all its plans and offers a presenter add-in for PowerPoint and Keynote on Mac. PollSlide works over Keynote, PowerPoint, Google Slides or a PDF with a QR on the slide; its PowerPoint add-in is in review with Microsoft.

How do PollSlide and Poll Everywhere prices compare?

PollSlide has a free plan for 25 participants per session and Pro at $12 a month ($10 billed annually). Poll Everywhere's free plan allows up to 40 people, and its Present plan is $10 a month or $120 billed annually, priced per organisation. Checked 7 October 2026.

Does my audience need to download an app?

No — with either tool. In PollSlide, people scan the QR or go to pollslide.com/join and answer in their phone's browser, with no app and no account.
