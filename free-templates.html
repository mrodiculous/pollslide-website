1. Pub Quiz Starter Pack

Pub Quiz Starter Pack

For: pub quiz hosts and trivia nights · One round, about 10 minutes to run
  1. What is the capital of Australia? (Answer: Canberra)
  2. Which planet in our solar system is known as the Red Planet? (Answer: Mars)
  3. Who painted the Mona Lisa? (Answer: Leonardo da Vinci)
  4. What is the largest ocean on Earth by surface area? (Answer: The Pacific Ocean)
  5. In what year did the Titanic sink? (Answer: 1912)
  6. What is the chemical symbol for gold? (Answer: Au)
  7. Which country originally gave the Statue of Liberty to the United States? (Answer: France)
  8. How many continents are there? (Answer: Seven)
  9. What is the smallest prime number? (Answer: 2)
  10. In Shakespeare's "Hamlet," what are the names of Hamlet's two childhood friends turned spies? (Answer: Rosencrantz and Guildenstern)

2. Team Meeting Icebreakers

Team Meeting Icebreakers

For: kicking off a work meeting or standup · 5 minutes, pick 2-3 or run all 10
  1. What's one thing you're looking forward to this week?
  2. If you could instantly master one new skill, what would it be?
  3. What's the best piece of advice you've ever received at work?
  4. Coffee, tea, or neither — what's your go-to?
  5. What's a small win from this week worth celebrating?
  6. If our team had a theme song right now, what would it be?
  7. What's one app or tool you couldn't do your job without?
  8. What's the most useful thing you've learned in the last month?
  9. Beach vacation or mountain retreat — which are you picking this year?
  10. What's one thing outside of work you're proud of right now?

3. Classroom Exit Ticket

Classroom Exit Ticket

For: the last 5 minutes of a lesson · quick formative check, 8 questions
  1. In one sentence, what was today's lesson mainly about?
  2. What's one new thing you learned today?
  3. On a scale of 1 to 5, how confident do you feel about today's topic?
  4. What's still confusing or unclear to you?
  5. Which part of today's class was most useful to you?
  6. Can you give one real-world example related to today's topic?
  7. If you could ask one question about today's lesson, what would it be?
  8. What's one thing you'd like more practice with before the next class?

4. New Hire / Onboarding Icebreaker

New Hire / Onboarding Icebreaker

For: a new team member's first team meeting · 8 questions, about 10 minutes
  1. What's your name, your new role, and one fun fact about you?
  2. What's something you're most excited to work on here?
  3. Coffee run or lunch spot — what's your go-to order?
  4. What's a hobby or interest outside of work people might not guess about you?
  5. What's one thing that would help you feel settled in during your first month?
  6. Where did you work (or what were you doing) right before this?
  7. What's your preferred way to communicate — chat, email, or a quick call?
  8. What's one question you have for the team right now?
