Questions

Is this basically Anki?

The scheduling idea is the same — proven spaced repetition, showing you a card just before you'd forget it. The difference is everything around it: a streak, a mastery bar, three games, no install for learners, and one-click conversion from a quiz you already made.

Do the games actually help me learn?

Yes — they use the same cards and the same scheduler as normal review. The only thing that changes is the framing and the scoring. The Board even adapts: high-value squares are the cards you know least well.

Do learners need an account?

No. Studying needs no login at all — progress is saved in their browser. If you are signed in (because it's your own material), progress syncs across your devices as a bonus.

Can I use it with a class?

Yes. Share the set and each learner studies at their own pace, while your dashboard shows overall mastery and the cards the group finds hardest.

Is it free?

Yes, the free plan runs real study sets. Paid plans lift limits on saved sets, audience size and AI question generation. See pricing.
