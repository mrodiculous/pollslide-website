Getting started · 2-minute video

Your first quiz in 2 minutes

Build a quiz or a poll, let Polly write the questions for you, add GIFs to questions and answers, and run it live — your audience answers on their phones.

The same steps, in writing

  1. Pick a poll or a quiz. Polls ask the room what it thinks. Quizzes have right answers, scores and a leaderboard. Choose the tab at the top left of the app.
  2. Create it. Click “+ Blank Quiz” or “+ Blank Poll” and give it a name. Everything saves automatically.
  3. Write a question. Type the question and the answers. In a quiz, tick the right answer with ✓.
  4. Add GIFs. Click “🔍 GIF” beside the question or beside any answer, search, and click the GIF you like.
  5. Let Polly write the rest. Click “✨ Ask Polly”, describe your topic and choose how many questions. Polly writes them with the right answers marked. Free accounts get 5 Polly drafts a month.
  6. GIFs for everything, in one go. Open the deck’s “⋯” menu, choose “GIFs”, tick questions and answers, and click “Find GIFs”. Review them before you present. Safe search is always on.
  7. Present. Press “▶ Present”, then “Present here”. Your audience scans the QR code with no app and no sign-up, and the results appear live. Press “Reveal” to show the answer.
Presenting from Keynote, PowerPoint or Google Slides? Copy the QR box onto your slide. Step-by-step guides for each app are in the help centre.
