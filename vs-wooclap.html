PollSlide vs Wooclap

Wooclap packs in 20+ question types and deep LMS/SSO integrations — but it asks you to build your session in its own editor, or let its add-in insert its own slides into PowerPoint or Google Slides. PollSlide asks for none of that: paste a QR box on the slide you already made, in any app, and results appear on top.

The short version

Wooclap is a mature, feature-rich platform for building a session inside its own event editor — or inserting its interactive slides into PowerPoint (native add-in) or Google Slides (browser extension) — with a big question-type library and real LMS/SSO wiring for schools and enterprise. PollSlide takes the opposite approach: your existing Keynote, PowerPoint, Slides or PDF deck never gets touched or converted — a QR box you paste onto any slide is the whole integration, and one free Mac companion floats results over whatever app you're already presenting from.
